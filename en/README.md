> From the 1956 Dartmouth Conference to Palantir helping Samsung boost yield — an open-source technical book that systematically explores the practical deployment of artificial intelligence in semiconductor manufacturing.

Semiconductor manufacturing is the most precise jewel on the crown of human industry. An advanced fab costs more than $15 billion, and a single wafer carries hundreds of billions of transistors. In an industry where every 1% improvement in yield means tens of millions of dollars in profit, AI is moving from the laboratory to the production line, from research papers to the fab floor.

This book takes the **three core departments of the fab** as its business mainline and the **three major schools of AI** as its technical mainline. The two threads interweave to systematically explain how AI technologies are truly deployed in semiconductor wafer fabs.

## Who Should Read This Book

- **Semiconductor engineers** — to understand how AI is being deployed in their own process, yield, manufacturing, and equipment domains
- **AI algorithm engineers** — to understand the particularities of semiconductor manufacturing and find valuable industrial AI application directions
- **Fab management and IT leaders** — to know which AI technologies are worth investing in and which are still at the proof-of-concept stage
- **Graduate students and researchers** — a reference that combines AI theory with semiconductor practice
- **Technology investors** — to understand the underlying logic of the semiconductor AI track
